# B2B Sales System for Redian (Diagnostic Centers)

Here is a complete, end-to-end B2B sales system for Redian, designed specifically for selling premium medical software to diagnostic centers. This draws heavily from Alex Hormozi’s *100M Leads* and standard B2B SaaS enterprise sales playbooks.

Because you are selling a high-ticket, mission-critical product, your sales process should not feel like "software sales." It should feel like **business consulting**.

---

### Phase 1: Prospecting (Finding the Leads)
Diagnostic centers are brick-and-mortar businesses, which makes them incredibly easy to find but hard to reach via traditional digital ads.

*   **The "Core 4" Lead Generation Methods (Hormozi):**
    1.  **Cold Outreach (Primary):** Scrape Google Maps, local medical directories, and health ministry registries for every diagnostic center in your target cities. 
    2.  **Warm Outreach:** Ask doctors you know to introduce you to lab owners.
    3.  **Content (Secondary):** Post case studies on LinkedIn targeting medical directors and hospital administrators.
    4.  **Paid Ads (Optional early on):** Run highly targeted Meta/LinkedIn ads to clinic owners with a Lead Magnet (e.g., *"How to stop reception revenue leaks in your diagnostic center"*).

### Phase 2: The Cold Approach (Getting the Meeting)
Lab owners and medical directors are busy. Your outreach must be short, punchy, and offer massive value. Do not sell the software here; **sell the meeting**.

*   **The Script Structure:** 
    *   *Acknowledge:* "Hi Dr. [Name], I know you're busy running the lab..."
    *   *The Hook (Identify the pain):* "...but I speak to a lot of clinic owners who are losing 10-15% of daily revenue to unrecorded tests and paper bottlenecks."
    *   *The Value (The pitch):* "We built a system that completely digitizes reception-to-lab workflow and works 100% offline so internet drops never stop your business."
    *   *The Ask:* "Do you have 15 minutes on Thursday for me to show you how we did this for [Competitor/Similar Clinic]?"
*   **The Medium:** Walk-ins (highly effective in this market), cold calls, or WhatsApp messages. Emails are often ignored in this demographic.

### Phase 3: The Discovery Call / Meeting (The Diagnosis)
When you get the meeting, **do not open your laptop and start showing features.** Treat this like a doctor treating a patient. Prescribe before you diagnose, and it's malpractice.

1.  **Ask Questions (Find the Bleeding Neck):**
    *   *"Walk me through what happens right now when a patient walks in."*
    *   *"How often do internet outages force you to use paper slips?"*
    *   *"How are you currently tracking end-of-day revenue vs. tests performed?"*
    *   *"How long does it take for a patient to get their printed results?"*
2.  **Quantify the Pain:** 
    *   *"If staff forgets to record 3 tests a day, how much money is that costing you a month?"* (Make them say the number out loud).

### Phase 4: The Pitch & Demo (The Prescription)
Now that you know their exact pain points, show them *only* the parts of Redian that solve those specific problems.

*   **The Framing:** "Based on what you told me about losing track of paper slips and internet issues, I want to show you three things our system does."
*   **The Demo:**
    1.  Show the Receptionist screen: How fast a patient is registered.
    2.  Show the Lab screen: How the test instantly appears for the technician.
    3.  **The "Magic" Moment:** Turn off your computer's Wi-Fi. Register a patient and show how the system still works perfectly offline (this will blow them away).
*   **Tie-down:** "Can you see how this would stop the revenue leaks at the front desk?"

### Phase 5: The Offer & Objection Handling
Present your Hormozi "Grand Slam Offer" confidently. 

*   **The Pitch:** *"To get this set up, we do a White-Glove Installation. We come to your clinic, install the local server, import all your specific tests, and train your staff. The setup is ₦750,000, and it's ₦75,000 a month for the cloud backup, maintenance, and 24/7 support."*
*   **Common Objections & How to Handle Them:**
    *   *Price is too high:* "I understand. But earlier you mentioned you're losing roughly ₦200,000 a month to unrecorded tests. This system plugs that hole on day one. It pays for itself in 4 weeks. Would you trade ₦75k a month to guarantee you collect all your revenue?"
    *   *My staff won't use it:* "That's exactly why we don't just hand you a software link. Our setup fee covers us coming in and physically training your staff until they are comfortable."
    *   *We already have a system:* "Does it work when the internet goes down? (Usually no). Let us run alongside it for 1 week and compare the speed."

### Phase 6: Closing and Onboarding (Delivering the Value)
*   **The Close:** "If you’re ready to stop the paper chaos, we have an installation slot open next Tuesday. Should we put you down for that?"
*   **The Contract:** Get a simple agreement signed and take a 50% deposit for the setup fee immediately to lock it in.
*   **Fulfillment:** Over-deliver on the onboarding. Bring donuts for the receptionists on training day. If the staff loves you, the clinic owner will never churn.

### Phase 7: The Flywheel (Referrals)
After 30 days, once they are seeing the results:
*   *"Dr. [Name], I'm glad the system has sped up your lab times. Who are two other medical directors you know that are still struggling with paper records?"* 

By using this consultative, high-touch system, you aren't selling a $50/mo subscription; you are selling a **business transformation** that commands a premium price.
